---
title: "Android: the foundation is in place"
date: 2026-10-15
summary: "Accessibility Service, rules engine, action executor and onboarding."
---

The groundwork of the Android app is done. None of it is visible yet, but everything else is built on top of it:

- **Accessibility Service skeleton** — listens only to the apps you choose to protect, with debounced events so it stays light on battery.
- **Rules engine** — a versioned, signed rule format, so detection can adapt when apps change their layouts without collecting anything from your phone.
- **Action executor and overlays** — the pieces that press back or draw a calm cover over a Reels shelf, with safeguards against loops.
- **Onboarding** — a step-by-step setup that explains the Accessibility permission in plain language *before* asking for it, plus phone-specific battery tips.

**Next:** detection rules for each platform (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat and more), then controls and stats.
