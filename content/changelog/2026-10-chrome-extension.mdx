---
title: "Reelless is coming to Chrome"
date: 2026-10-19
summary: "Two products, one goal: the Android app and a Chrome extension."
---

Reelless will be two things: the **Android app** for your phone, and a **Chrome extension** for your computer. Work on the extension has started alongside the app.

The extension will:

- Hide Shorts, Reels and recommendation feeds on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, Reddit and LinkedIn.
- Offer the same controls as the app: Search-only YouTube, Friends-first Facebook, keyword filters, schedules and allowances.
- Use the same signed detection rules as the app, run only on the sites you enable, and load no remote code.
- Work fully without an account. Like the app, it won't block ads.

There's no iPhone version planned — Apple doesn't let apps act inside other apps. If you use an iPhone, the Chrome extension can still help on your computer.

You can now choose Android, Chrome or both when you [join early access](/#waitlist).
