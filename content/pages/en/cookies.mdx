## No tracking or advertising cookies

The Reelless website does **not** use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, social-media pixels, or Google Analytics. We don't build profiles of visitors and we don't follow you around the web.

## Cookieless analytics

When analytics are enabled, we use a privacy-friendly, **cookieless** tool (a self-hosted Umami or Plausible instance). It counts page views and referrers in aggregate, without cookies, without storing personal data, and without cross-site tracking. It also respects your browser's Do Not Track setting.

## What may be stored in your browser

A few small items may be stored in your browser to make the site work, and none of them are used for tracking:

| Item | Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `theme` | Local storage | Remembers whether you chose light, dark or system theme. |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Cookie (functional) | Remembers the language you picked, so the site opens in it next time. |

These are strictly necessary or preference items, so no consent banner is required for them under most privacy laws.

## Spam protection

If our forms use **Cloudflare Turnstile** to tell people from bots, Cloudflare may process limited technical information when you submit a form. Turnstile is designed not to use cookies for tracking. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.
