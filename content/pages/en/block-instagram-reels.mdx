## The app you need, wrapped around the feed you don't

Most people don't open Instagram to watch Reels. They open it to reply to a message, check a friend's Story, or post a photo. But the Reels tab sits in the middle of the bottom bar, Reels appear in the feed, and a reel shared in a DM opens into a full-screen player where the next one is a swipe away.

You can't really leave Instagram if your friends are there. You can make Reels stop pulling you in.

## What Instagram itself lets you do

Instagram's controls move around, so treat this as a snapshot at the time of writing:

- **"Not interested"** on a reel tunes what you see next, but doesn't reduce how much you see.
- **Snoozing suggested posts** in your feed hides suggestions for a while; it doesn't touch the Reels tab or the player.
- **Daily time reminders** in *Your activity* nudge you after a set time each day. They're easy to dismiss.
- **Sensitive content controls** change *what* kind of reels you're shown, not *whether* you're shown them.

There's no switch that turns Reels off. If there were, this page wouldn't need to exist.

## What Reelless blocks on Instagram

- **The Reels player.** Whether it opens from the Reels tab, a post in your feed, or a link, Reelless closes it as soon as it appears.
- **The Reels tab.** Tapping it does nothing.
- **The Explore grid.** Explore is mostly reels these days, so it's covered by default. You can turn this off if you use Explore for search.
- **Suggested reels in your feed.** Rows of suggested reels are covered with a calm panel.

What keeps working: **direct messages, Stories, your home feed, posting photos and Stories, comments, your profile, search.**

## One and done

The hardest case is the reel a friend sends you. You want to see it — it's part of the conversation — but the moment you finish, the next reel starts. With **One and done**, Reelless lets that single reel play. Swipe for the next, and the player closes, dropping you back into the chat.

## How to set it up

1. Install Reelless and select **Instagram** as a protected app.
2. Leave **Reels player**, **Reels tab** and **Explore** on (the defaults), or adjust them.
3. Turn on Reelless in **Accessibility settings** when prompted. Reelless shows you a plain-language explanation first; the same text is on our [How it works](/how-it-works) page.
4. Open Instagram, tap the Reels tab, and notice that nothing happens. That's the point.

If you want a softer approach, set a **daily short-form allowance** instead — for example 10 minutes of Reels a day — and let Reelless enforce it.

## Your account stays yours

Reelless never asks for your Instagram password, never logs in, and never touches your account. It doesn't read your messages. Recognition happens entirely on your phone and nothing about your screen leaves it. Our [Accessibility Service disclosure](/accessibility-disclosure) explains this in detail.
